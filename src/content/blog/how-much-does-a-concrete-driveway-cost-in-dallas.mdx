## Average Concrete Driveway Costs in Dallas

If you're planning a new concrete driveway in Dallas, expect to pay between **$6 and $15 per square foot** depending on the size, thickness, finish, and site conditions. For a standard two-car driveway (roughly 400–600 square feet), that puts the total project cost between **$2,400 and $9,000**.

## What Affects the Price?

Several factors influence concrete driveway pricing in the Dallas area:

### Size and Thickness

A standard residential driveway is 4 inches thick. If you park heavier vehicles like trucks or RVs, you may need 5–6 inches of thickness, which increases material costs.

### Site Preparation

If your existing driveway needs demolition and removal, or if the soil requires grading and compaction, site prep adds to the total cost. Properties with poor drainage may also need additional grading work.

### Finish Type

A basic broom finish is the most affordable option. Stamped concrete, exposed aggregate, and colored concrete add visual appeal but increase the cost per square foot.

### Reinforcement

Steel rebar or fiber mesh reinforcement adds strength and longevity. While it increases upfront cost, it reduces the risk of cracking and extends the life of your driveway.

## Getting the Best Value

The cheapest quote is not always the best value. Look for contractors who:

- Provide a detailed written estimate
- Are licensed and insured
- Can show examples of past work
- Explain their process for site prep, reinforcement, and finishing
- Offer a clear timeline for your project
